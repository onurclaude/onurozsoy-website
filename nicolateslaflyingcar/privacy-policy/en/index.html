NIKOLA TESLA FLYING CAR · ANDROID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Türkçe oku

This policy explains which data is processed when you use the Nikola Tesla Flying Car Android game (package name com.onurozsoy.nikolateslaflyingcar), for what purposes it is used, and what your rights are. The game has no accounts or sign-in. However, because the game uses Google's Firebase and AdMob services, certain data about your device and your use of the game is processed through these services. We therefore do not claim that the game processes no personal data.

1. Developer and contact details

The game is developed by Onur Özsoy, who is also the data controller for the processing described in this policy.

  • Email: onur@ozsoyonur.com
  • Website: ozsoyonur.com/nicolateslaflyingcar

2. Purpose and how the game works

Nikola Tesla Flying Car is a physics-based 3D game in which you launch a rocket-powered car from a ramp, control it in the air and try to land it safely on a target landing platform. The game contains 20 stages that unlock in order, and successful landings earn 1 to 3 stars. Each stage starts with 10 attempts. When you run out of attempts you can optionally watch a rewarded ad to get +1 attempt; if no ad is available, 1 attempt is restored for free after a waiting period.

3. No accounts

The game has no user registration, sign-in or player account. You are not asked for your name, email address, phone number or a password. There is no player server that stores your game progress.

4. Game progress on your device

Your game progress is stored only on your device, using Unity's local storage mechanism (PlayerPrefs). This includes unlocked stages, the highest number of stars earned in each stage, successful completions, remaining attempts, the free-attempt waiting timer and related game state.

This progress data is not sent to any server operated by the developer and is not synchronised to the cloud. If you uninstall the game or clear the app's data in Android settings, this data is deleted and cannot be restored. If your device's own backup features back up app data, that backup is subject to the services of Google or your device manufacturer.

5. Firebase Analytics

We use Google's Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve stage difficulty, the attempt system and the ad experience.

Events sent by the game

For example, events are recorded when the game starts, a stage starts, a stage is completed or failed, attempts run out, a rewarded ad is offered and completed, an attempt is granted as a reward, an interstitial ad is shown, the player levels up, and a required update is shown (e.g. game_start, stage_complete, stage_fail, attempts_exhausted, reward_ad_completed, interstitial_shown, forced_update_required). These events may include details such as the stage number, number of stars, stage duration, failure reason and the app version code.

The game does not add your name, email address or phone number to these events.

Data collected automatically by the SDK

The Firebase Analytics SDK also collects certain data automatically. According to Google, this may include the app instance ID, device model, operating system version, language and region settings, app version, first-open and session information, and approximate location derived from the IP address. Where available and not restricted on the device, the Android advertising ID may also be collected. This data is processed by Google; as the developer, we see it in aggregated reports rather than in a way that identifies you.

6. Firebase Crashlytics crash reports

We use Google's Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes and technical errors. When a crash or a logged error occurs, the SDK may send Google the error details (stack trace), error logs, the app version, technical device information such as device model, operating system version and memory/disk state, and installation identifiers associated with the report (Crashlytics installation UUID, Firebase installation ID). These reports do not intentionally include personal information from game content.

7. Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to manage certain game settings remotely and to check versions. The game retrieves values from Firebase such as whether a forced update is enabled and the minimum supported and latest version codes (force_update_enabled, minimum_version_code, latest_version_code). When necessary, players using older versions may be asked to update the game. This does not require a player account; to deliver the configuration, Firebase processes technical data such as the Firebase installation ID and app/device information.

8. Google AdMob ads

The game is free and supported by ads, which are served by Google AdMob. The game uses two ad formats:

  • Rewarded ads: When you run out of attempts in a stage, you can optionally watch an ad to get +1 attempt. You never have to watch it. The attempt is granted once the ad has been completed and the ad service has confirmed the reward.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ad: Shown once, the first time you move from the first main level to the second main level.

To serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, where permitted, personalise ads, Google may process the Android advertising ID (where available and not restricted), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app package name and version, language and network information), ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completion) and diagnostic data. For more information on how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

9. Ad consent and privacy choices

The game integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent/choice form is intended to be shown before ads are requested, where required by applicable rules. In that form you can accept or refuse personalised ads and related data processing; if you refuse, limited or non-personalised ads may be shown.

The game menu contains a privacy choices button; in regions where the consent form is required, you can reopen it there to change or withdraw your choices.

Note: As of the date of this policy, the content of the consent form is still being configured in the Google AdMob account. Once published, the form will be shown in the relevant regions.

Regardless of your region, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads). You can manage ad preferences for your Google account at My Ad Center.

Your ad consent choices do not automatically switch Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics processing on or off.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress on your device: Kept on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Apps → Nikola Tesla Flying Car → Storage → Clear data deletes it.
  • Firebase Analytics: Data is retained according to the retention setting of the Firebase project and Google's policies. Aggregated, anonymised reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reports are retained for the period Google specifies for Crashlytics (see Privacy and Security in Firebase).
  • Google AdMob: Advertising data is handled according to Google's own retention policies (see How Google retains data).

Uninstalling and reinstalling the app resets the app instance and installation identifiers Firebase uses for this device. If you want data that has already been sent to Google to be deleted, contact us; we will fulfil your request to the extent that we can match it to an identifier of your device using the tools Firebase provides. Because there are no accounts, we may ask for additional information to match your request to your data, and in some cases matching may not be possible.

12. Your rights under KVKK and GDPR

Under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information about such processing; to learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used in line with that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom data is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request deletion or destruction of data when the legal conditions are met; to request that such actions be notified to third parties to whom data was transferred; to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, to the extent applicable you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, including the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

13. Data requests

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, email onur@ozsoyonur.com. Please mention the game name (Nikola Tesla Flying Car) and describe your request clearly. We aim to respond as soon as possible and within the period required by KVKK (no later than 30 days).

14. Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly ask children for personal information such as a name, email address or contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe the data described above has been processed while your child used the game and you would like it deleted, please contact us.
